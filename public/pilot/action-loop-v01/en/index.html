Reflection, not instruction

Three possible exit directions

Important origin note. Creator, Coach and Challenger are not original roles from Karpman's Drama Triangle. They are an alternative role set proposed by David Emerald's The Empowerment Dynamic (TED*). Here they are used only as language for self-reflection.
Victim → Creator direction

More choice

Shift from “what is this situation doing to me?” toward “what can I do now that is within my control?”

Rescuer → Coach direction

More questions and boundaries

Do not take over another person's responsibility; help them keep it.

Persecutor → Challenger direction

Clear, respectful challenge

Talk about specific behaviour, impact and expectations, not the person's worth.

From insight to experiment

Did this reflection give you an idea to try something differently?

Not “what should you change?” Only whether one real situation came to mind where you would like to try a different response.
